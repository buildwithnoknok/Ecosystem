match your printer + material: 0 = normal, − tighter, + looser (e.g. +0.1 for a less-accurate printer or stiff PLA)
Two-cover sandwich, a bay per buzzer. Cable the module outside, insert it from below into the top cover (buzzer up, against the grille), then snap on the bottom cover — its ribs press the PCB up and clamp it (no PCB snap-lips). Cables lay into the W/E notches (open at the seam). Pop the bottom cover to remove the module. Both covers print flat, side by side.
